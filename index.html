How it plays

The part in your hand is the part you fight with

01 Build

Grab guns, bombs, pods, legs and bodies off a real workbench and snap them onto your mech. It reacts to every new part.

02 Launch

Aim a first-person capsule cannon, pick your landing spot and fire. Your mech bursts out ready to fight.

03 Fight

Drive it from ringside with a fighting-game HUD and a live stadium big screen. Best of three, with a kill cam after every K.O.

Features

Everything in the box

  • 30+ parts across guns, bombs, pods, legs and bodies, built by hand
  • Capsule launch from a first-person cannon at the start of every round
  • Ringside battles with a fighting-game HUD and a live big screen
  • Mirror mode VS intros: your mech copies your head and arms
  • Killer's-eye kill cam replays every K.O.
  • Quick Battle: random builds, 1v1 up to 3v3 and 1v4, right-stick lock-on
  • Rival Ladder: SPARKY, BOLT, HALO, VIPER and the illegal champion KING CROWN
  • Online 1v1: LAN now, internet rooms coming
  • Story chapter: Haven, a sewer and pipe infiltration, a security test pit and an EDEN ambush
  • Six arenas with pits, conveyors, presses, shredders, laser sweepers and overload switches
  • Comfortable: a stationary viewpoint, seated or standing
